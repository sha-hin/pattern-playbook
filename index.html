Pattern Playbook

For Laravel developers

Design patterns in Laravel, inside a real PMS

All 23 Gang of Four patterns and 15 modern ones, each applied to the business logic of a vacation-rental property management system: bookings, pricing, payments, channel sync, guest messaging. When Laravel already ships a pattern, you see how Laravel builds it inside.
