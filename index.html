Pattern Playbook

Advanced Laravel, in 7 days

One rental platform, 42 production incidents

Follow Nordic Stays, a vacation-rental property management system, from its first booking to its busiest day. Every scene starts with something breaking in production. You read the broken code, fix it, and only then learn the name of the pattern you just used. 34 patterns arrive as real fixes inside the story; the 4 a PMS doesn’t need are built on their own at the end. That’s all 23 Gang of Four patterns and 15 modern ones, with the Laravel engineering around them.
