Pattern Playbook

Advanced Laravel, in 7 days

One rental platform, 44 production incidents

Follow Nordic Stays, a vacation-rental property management system, from its first booking to its busiest day. Every scene starts with something breaking in production. You read the broken code, fix it the way a senior Laravel team would, and see the pattern and the framework internals behind the fix.

3. The fixProduction code, a short “why” on every important line.
4. The nameThe pattern you just wrote, how Laravel uses it, then the tests, folded.
