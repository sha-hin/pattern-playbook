Pattern Playbook

A design patterns course for PHP developers

Learn design patterns by fixing one real app

Evalu8 is a school evaluation platform that starts as one messy PHP script. Every chapter brings a real ticket, a bug, a new requirement or a scaling problem, and fixes it with the pattern made for it. By the end, the same codebase uses all 23 Gang of Four patterns and 15 modern ones.
